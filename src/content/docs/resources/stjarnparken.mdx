The current skatepark has a ramp that's in poor condition and hard to use due to its measurements.
The ground is asphalt, which makes falling a bit riskier.
The skatepark has a lot of gravel and sticks nearby that kids grab and play with in the skatepark.
The street part has some bad angles and measurements making it hard to use.
