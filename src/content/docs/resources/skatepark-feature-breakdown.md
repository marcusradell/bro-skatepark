## Notes & Material Specifications

1. **Concrete Quality:** All transition and flat surfaces utilize C35/45 wet-mix shotcrete troweled to a smooth, high-density finish for minimal rolling resistance and maximum weather resistance.
2. **Coping Standards:**
   * **Pool Coping:** High-density concrete pool coping blocks installed in the bowl deep end.
   * **Steel Coping:** Schedule 80 hot-dip galvanized steel pipe anchored with concrete lugs.
3. **Street Surfaces:** Ledges feature solid natural granite edging to eliminate chipping and minimize maintenance costs over time.
4. **Exclusions in this Table:** General earthworks, mass excavation, sub-base preparation, geotechnics, and contingency reserves are calculated in the overall project budget (~5.35 MSEK additional).
