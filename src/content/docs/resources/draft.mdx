# Citizen Proposal: Construction of a Modern Concrete Skatepark in Stjärnparken, Bro

**Recipient:** Municipal Council / Culture and Leisure Committee, Upplands-Bro Municipality  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Subject:** Formal citizen proposal for a 1,200 m² cast-in-place concrete skatepark in Stjärnparken (Råbyplan), Bro, modeled after Huvudsta Skatepark in Solna.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Motion

### Summary
This citizen proposal advocates for the construction of a **1,200 square meter** cast-in-place concrete skatepark in Stjärnparken, Bro . Inspired by Solna’s Huvudsta Skatepark, the facility balances technical quality—featuring a multi-depth bowl, flow landscape, and street progression zone—with fiscal responsibility and site adaptation .

The total estimated investment is **10.6 million SEK**, with external co-funding possibilities from RF-SISU (up to 1.5 MSEK) and the Swedish Inheritance Fund (*Allmänna Arvsfonden*), bringing the net municipal cost down to approximately **9.1 MSEK** .

### Formal Motion
The Municipal Council of Upplands-Bro is urged to resolve:
1. **To** mandate the Culture & Leisure Committee and the Community Development Committee to project and construct a 1,200 m² concrete skatepark in Stjärnparken, Bro .
2. **To** allocate 10.6 MSEK in the municipal capital budget, with instructions to actively pursue external grants (RF-SISU/Arvsfonden) .
3. **To** involve local youth and skaters in the detailed design process .

## 2. Key Site & Design Specifications

### Spatial Layout & Zoning (1,200 m²)
* **Zone A: Bowl Landscape (450 m²):** Multi-depth bowl with shallow (1.2–1.4 m) and deep (2.3–2.5 m) ends, pool coping, steel coping, spine, and hip .
* **Zone B: Flow & Transitions (350 m²):** Pumping bumps, volcanoes, and carved turns connecting street and bowl areas smoothly .
* **Zone C: Street & Progression (300 m²):** Low granite ledges (15–30 cm), manual pads, slappy curbs, and bank-to-bank with stairs and flat-down rail .
* **Zone D: Amenities & Periphery (100 m²):** Concrete/wooden benches, waste bins, bicycle racks, and 4–5 LED light masts with automatic timers .
* **Buffer & Environmental Features:** A 3-meter smooth concrete buffer prevents zone collisions . A planted earth mound in the west dampens noise toward residential areas, and a biosvale in the south manages rainwater runoff .

## 4. Strategic Benefits

* **Public Health & Inclusion:** Free, non-organized physical activity accessible to all youth regardless of club affiliation or financial background .
* **Long-Term Durability:** Plats-cast concrete offers a 30–50 year lifespan with minimal maintenance compared to wooden or steel modular ramps .
* **Safety & Cohesion:** Central park integration, bright LED lighting, and clear sightlines enhance evening safety and social vibrancy in Bro .
