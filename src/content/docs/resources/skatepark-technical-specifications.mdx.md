---
title: Technical Specifications for Skatepark Features
---

Detailed design, physical dimensions, angular geometries, user target demographics, and contextual integration requirements for each individual feature within the skatepark.

---

## 1. Deep End Bowl Section

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Depth: 2.30 m to 2.50 m. Flat bottom width: 4.50 m minimum. Top deck width: 1.50 m perimeter safety walkway.
* **Angles & Radius:** Transition radius: 2.40 m to 2.60 m. Vertical pitch: 0.15 m vertical section (90°) directly under the lip coping.
* **Target Audience:** Advanced skateboarders, BMX riders, and experienced transition skaters.
* **Contextual Needs:** Requires high-density pool coping blocks set flush with a 6 mm reveal for authentic pool grinding sound and feel. Located away from entry points to prevent accidental entry by beginners. Deep drainage sump with heavy-duty cast iron grate at lowest point.

---

## 2. Shallow End Bowl Section

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Depth: 1.20 m to 1.40 m. Flat bottom width: 3.50 m minimum.
* **Angles & Radius:** Transition radius: 2.10 m (smooth, non-vertical curve). Pitch: Maximum 75° at top coping.
* **Target Audience:** Beginner to intermediate transition skaters learning drop-ins, carves, and lip tricks.
* **Contextual Needs:** Seamlessly connected to the deep end via waterfall transition. Fitted with 60 mm schedule 80 hot-dip galvanized steel coping. High sightline visibility from peripheral seating for parental supervision.

---

## 3. Spine & Hip Transition Feature

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Height: 1.20 m. Spine apex width: 150 mm steel back-to-back coping. Hip angles: 45° and 90° corner transfers.
* **Angles & Radius:** Transition radius: 2.10 m matching shallow bowl curve.
* **Target Audience:** Intermediate to advanced skaters and BMX riders developing air transfers and direction switches.
* **Contextual Needs:** Positioned between transition pockets to facilitate continuous flow without foot pushing. Smooth coping welds and burnished concrete finish to minimize drag during transfers.

---

## 4. Pumping Bumps / Speed Humps

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Height: 0.35 m to 0.40 m. Base footprint: 2.20 m long × 1.80 m wide.
* **Angles & Radius:** Smooth sinusoidal curve (convex apex, concave toe transitions) with a 3.50 m gentle radius.
* **Target Audience:** All skill levels (beginners learn speed generation; advanced riders maintain momentum).
* **Contextual Needs:** Positioned in flat-bottom transition runs to maintain rider momentum without kicking. Surface burnished smooth to prevent speed loss. Smoothly blended base edges to eliminate trip hazards.

---

## 5. Central Concrete Volcano

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Apex height: 0.80 m. Top platform diameter: 0.60 m flat apex. Base diameter: 3.80 m.
* **Angles & Radius:** 360-degree continuous transition radius of 2.20 m.
* **Target Audience:** Intermediate to advanced skaters, inline skaters, and BMX riders.
* **Contextual Needs:** Functions as a central traffic hub allowing 360-degree carving, transfers, and directional changes. Requires 4.00 m of clear run-out area around all sides. Top flat fitted with flush steel edge or rubberized cap.

---

## 6. Open Carve Corner Quarterpipes

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Height: 1.40 m to 1.60 m. Arc length: 6.00 m along outer coping edge.
* **Angles & Radius:** Transition radius: 2.30 m. Coping reveal: 5 mm top, 8 mm front.
* **Target Audience:** All skill levels (carving for beginners, wallrides and lip tricks for advanced).
* **Contextual Needs:** Functions as a speed retainer at the perimeter of the flow section, turning riders back toward central features. Fitted with 60 mm galvanized steel coping anchored by welded rebar lugs.

---

## 7. Granite Ledges

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Height: 0.15 m (low ledge) to 0.30 m (high ledge). Width: 0.45 m top surface. Length: 3.60 m to 4.80 m.
* **Angles & Radius:** 90° squared grinding edges with 5 mm radius chamfer on solid natural granite blocks.
* **Target Audience:** Beginner to advanced street skaters.
* **Contextual Needs:** Crafted from solid polished granite slabs to prevent concrete chipping and eliminate the need for surface wax. Requires 12.00 m clear approach run and 8.00 m landing zone.

---

## 8. Flat Manual Pads

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Height: 0.15 m to 0.20 m. Top surface width: 1.20 m. Length: 3.60 m.
* **Angles & Radius:** 90° edges fitted with 50 mm × 50 mm × 5 mm steel angle iron along grinding sides.
* **Target Audience:** Beginner to intermediate skaters practicing balance, manual tricks, and low impact pop-ons/pop-offs.
* **Contextual Needs:** Placed parallel to primary street lines with smooth concrete approach and landing zones. Angle iron recessed flush with concrete top surface for seamless wheel transition.

---

## 9. Slappy Curbs

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Height: 0.15 m. Width: 0.30 m base tapering to 0.20 m top. Length: 3.00 m straight and S-curved segments.
* **Angles & Radius:** 45° angled front face with rounded top lip (radius 30 mm).
* **Target Audience:** All skill levels, especially low-impact street skaters and beginners learning grind mechanics.
* **Contextual Needs:** Steel-troweled smooth concrete with anti-graffiti sealant. Located in low-speed perimeter zones where skaters can practice without interfering with high-speed transition runs.

---

## 10. Bank-to-Bank w/ Stairs & Flat-Down Rail Complex

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Overall footprint: 12.00 m long × 4.50 m wide. Bank height: 1.20 m. Stair set: 4-step arrangement (tread 0.30 m, riser 0.15 m). Gap width: 1.80 m.
* **Angles & Radius:** Bank slope angle: 22° flat incline. Rail slope: 22° parallel to stair slope.
* **Target Audience:** Intermediate to advanced street skaters.
* **Contextual Needs:** Forms the central technical street obstacle. Clear sightlines are required from both approach directions to prevent collisions between stair-jumpers and bank-riders.

---

## 11. Galvanized Steel Flat & Round Rails

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Round rail diameter: 50 mm O.D. Flat rail width: 60 mm × 40 mm rectangular tube. Height: 0.35 m (flat rail) to 0.45 m (down rail). Length: 3.60 m.
* **Angles & Radius:** Rounded rail ends curved down to ground level (radius 150 mm) to eliminate impalement hazards.
* **Target Audience:** Intermediate to advanced street skaters and BMX riders.
* **Contextual Needs:** Hot-dip galvanized heavy-walled steel pipe set directly into concrete foundations (not surface-bolted) for maximum structural rigidity and low noise output.

---

## 12. Cast-in-Place Concrete Benches

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Seat height: 0.45 m. Seat depth: 0.50 m. Length: 2.40 m.
* **Angles & Radius:** Ergonomic 5° backward tilt on seat surface with 15 mm rounded front seating edge.
* **Target Audience:** Spectators, resting athletes, families, and park visitors.
* **Contextual Needs:** Integrated wooden slat tops (treated larch/oak) for thermal comfort during cold weather. Positioned outside safety drop-zones but within sight of all main riding areas.

---

## 13. Integrated Wooden Bleachers

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Total height: 1.50 m (3 tiered rows). Tread depth: 0.75 m per tier. Width: 6.00 m overall.
* **Angles & Radius:** Tiered step structure (90° vertical risers, horizontal treads).
* **Target Audience:** Event spectators, local youth groups, parents, and competition judges.
* **Contextual Needs:** Situated along the elevated western perimeter mound to provide an unobstructed overview of the entire park while serving as a natural barrier between riding zones and walkways.

---

## 14. High-Efficiency LED Lighting Masts

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Mast height: 10.00 m to 12.00 m. Luminaire tilt angle: 15° downward inclination.
* **Angles & Radius:** Asymmetric light distribution cones providing a minimum 50 lux uniform coverage across all riding surfaces.
* **Target Audience:** All park users during twilight and evening hours.
* **Contextual Needs:** Automated timer controls with dusk-to-dawn sensors (shutoff at 22:00 to comply with municipal noise ordinances). Mounted outside concrete riding areas behind safety barriers.

---

## 15. Waste Bins & Bike Racks Set

* **Measurements & Dimensions:** Bike rack capacity: 10 bicycles (0.70 m spacing per slot). Bin volume: 100 liters with integrated ash receptacle.
* **Angles & Radius:** Standard vertical post-and-loop steel bike racks.
* **Target Audience:** All park visitors, commuters, and daily users.
* **Contextual Needs:** Constructed from vandal-resistant powder-coated steel anchored into concrete footings. Located at the main entry plaza adjacent to lighting masts for safety and visibility.
