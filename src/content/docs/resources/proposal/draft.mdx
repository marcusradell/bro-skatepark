## Executive Summary & Motion

### Summary
This citizen proposal advocates for the construction of a concrete skatepark in Bro.
The facility focuses on bowls and transitions and should try to be inclusive for skateboarders, kickbikers, rollerskaters, and BMX riders.

The draft is working on an approximate cost of 15MSEK and a size of 1500m2, and will be updated later in the process.

### Suggested Zones

* **Zone A: Bowl Landscape:** Multi-depth bowl with shallow (1.2–1.4 m) and deep (2.3–2.5 m) ends, pool coping, steel coping, spine, and hip.
* **Zone B: Flow & Transitions:** Pumping bumps, volcanoes, and carved turns connecting street and bowl areas smoothly .
* **Zone C: Beginner's area:** A flatground place for beginner's to practice roll-ins, drop-ins, slappies, mini spine tricks, and tricks in a mellow mini ramp.
* **Zone D: Amenities & Periphery:** Concrete/wooden benches, waste bins, bicycle racks, and 4–5 LED light masts with automatic timers.
* **Buffer & Environmental Features:** A 3-meter smooth concrete buffer prevents zone collisions . A planted earth mound in the west dampens noise toward residential areas, and a biosvale in the south manages rainwater runoff.

## Strategic Benefits

* **Low maintenance cost** Plats-cast concrete offers a 30–50 year lifespan with minimal maintenance compared to wooden or steel modular ramps.
* **Socially flexible** Young people meet old people. Beginners meet experienced skaters.
* **Visitors:** Many skaters commute to get to a good park.
* **Builds community:** With a good park, skaters will get together to form a skate community.
